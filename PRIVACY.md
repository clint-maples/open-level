# Privacy policy

Open Level is a digital level for Android, published by Clint Maples.

## Sensors

While the app is open, it reads one motion sensor on the device:

- The gravity sensor (`Sensor.TYPE_GRAVITY`) when the device has one.
- The accelerometer (`Sensor.TYPE_ACCELEROMETER`) only when a gravity sensor is not available.

It does not use the gyroscope, the rotation vector, the game rotation vector, the geomagnetic rotation vector, the magnetometer, or any other sensor. The reading is filtered on the device and shown as tilt. It is not stored as a history and it is not sent anywhere.

## Data

Open Level collects no data and shares no data. It does not request the internet permission and it has no network access. There are no ads, no analytics, and no accounts.

Calibration (the zero you set), whether haptic ticks are on, and whether angles are shown in degrees or percent slope are saved in app-private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app removes them. Nothing is uploaded.
